About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Finance Department at the Tippie College of Business, University of Iowa. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Boston College in 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of health economics and industrial organization. I study health insurance markets, healthcare providers, and the design of public health insurance programs, with a particular focus on Medicare Advantage.
