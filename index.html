About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Finance Department at the Tippie College of Business, University of Iowa. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Boston College in 2026.

My research lies at the intersection of health economics and industrial organization. I study the design of public health insurance programs, vertical integration in health care markets, and spillovers across different public programs.
